# Discussion / mechanism / interpretation sections

Loads on top of [`../writing_style.md`](../writing_style.md) (general
baseline) and [`body.md`](body.md) (body baseline).

---

- **Reconcile competing explanations.** When the data are consistent
  with more than one mechanism, name the alternatives and adjudicate —
  or admit you cannot. Silence on alternatives reads as evasion.
- **No rhetorical persuasion in the body.** "Clearly", "it is evident",
  "one cannot deny", appeals to authority mid-argument — flag and
  remove. State the claim and the evidence; let the reader judge. See
  `validate-section`'s robot-body persuasion check.
- **Anchor each claim to specific evidence.** "Figure 4 shows…",
  "Column 3 of Table 5 indicates…" — make the pointer specific so the
  reader can check.
- **Acknowledge what the design cannot pin down.** Which alternatives
  cannot be ruled out, and what would be required to rule them out
  (more data, a different design, a structural model). See
  [`body.md`](body.md)'s acknowledge-weaknesses rule.
- **Don't editorialize the data.** "The temporal evidence is starkest"
  → flag and rewrite. State the finding; let the numbers carry the
  weight. See general §2.
- **Hedged descriptive phrasing is fine.** "The data are consistent
  with…", "this pattern suggests…" — these state without overclaiming
  and are appropriate in discussion. The robot-body rule flags
  *persuasion*, not appropriately-hedged statement.
