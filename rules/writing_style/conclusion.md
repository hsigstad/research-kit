# Conclusion sections

Loads on top of [`../writing_style.md`](../writing_style.md) (general
baseline). Conclusions do **not** inherit from `body.md` — different
rules apply.

---

- **Short.** Two short paragraphs is fine; one is often better. Don't
  restate all the findings — one statement in the abstract, one in the
  introduction, and once in the body should be enough (Cochrane).
- **Don't write your future research agenda.** "We leave X for future
  research" reads as filler; readers care about what's in the paper.
- **Don't speculate.** Save speculation for the discussion or for
  `thinking.md`.
- **Close with implications, not summary.** What does this paper teach
  us? What changes for theory, policy, or further research?
- **No new claims.** A conclusion that introduces a new finding or
  interpretation that didn't appear in the body is a sign the body is
  in the wrong place.
- **No throat-clearing.** "In conclusion, we have shown…" — drop
  "in conclusion"; the section heading already says it. See general §8.
