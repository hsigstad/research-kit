# Appendix sections

Loads on top of [`../writing_style.md`](../writing_style.md) (general
baseline). Appendices follow the same prose rules as the main text but
have additional content rules.

---

- **Same standards as the main text.** An appendix that is sloppy
  signals that the main text might be too. Captions self-contained,
  numbers discussed, robust to skim-reading.
- **What belongs here.** Robustness checks not central to the main
  argument; alternative specifications; technical proofs; data
  appendices that catalog rather than analyze; extended descriptive
  panels; methodological details that would distract in the body.
- **What does not belong here.** Anything the paper's main argument
  depends on — that goes in the body. Appendices are skippable; if a
  reader cannot follow the main argument without reading the appendix,
  it is not actually appendix material.
- **Index from the main text.** Each appendix section should be
  referenced from the body where its content is relevant. An appendix
  nobody cites is dead weight (Cochrane: "you can put it on your
  webpage").
- **Self-contained organization.** An appendix labeled "A" should be
  navigable on its own — own table of contents if long, own subsection
  numbering, own caption-only-readable tables.
- **Online appendix vs. in-paper appendix.** If the journal allows an
  online supplement, prefer that for material the median reader will
  not need; reserve the in-paper appendix for material a working
  referee will want at hand.
