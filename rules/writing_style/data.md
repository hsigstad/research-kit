# Data sections

Loads on top of [`../writing_style.md`](../writing_style.md) (general
baseline) and [`body.md`](body.md) (body baseline).

---

- **Provenance.** Where the data come from (source, vintage, version),
  how they were obtained, and any access conditions. Cite primary
  sources, not aggregator URLs.
- **Sample restrictions stated upfront.** Every "we restrict to…" is
  motivated, with the resulting sample size reported. Make the
  restrictions a single readable list, not a buried prose chain.
- **Variable construction.** For each constructed variable, the
  definition and the underlying raw fields. Cross-reference the data
  documentation in `docs/data.md` rather than duplicating it.
- **Units and coverage.** Time period, geographic scope, unit of
  observation. Make a skimming reader able to picture what one row is.
- **Don't replicate the codebook.** A long table of variable means is
  rarely the right move; a focused descriptive panel that motivates the
  empirical strategy is.
- **Limitations honestly.** Selection issues, measurement error,
  coverage gaps — name them here rather than burying them in footnotes.
- **Define foreign-language terms on first use.** *câmara* (courtroom),
  *relator* (first-voting judge), etc. See general §6.
- **Anchor descriptive numbers to the empirical strategy.** A summary
  statistic that does not eventually motivate a regression or test is
  filler; cut.
